THE STANDARD

What's inside
every row.

31 data columns per business, grouped into six research layers. Where data can't be verified, the cell says so — Not Found or Not verified, never a guess.

Business Identity

Legal and trade name, category, service area, address, years in business, license signals where public.

Contact Layer

Phone, email, website, contact forms, social profiles. Conflicting numbers are flagged, not silently picked.

Website Audit

Does the site load? Mobile-friendly? SSL? Last updated? A dead or hijacked site is your strongest rebuild pitch — it's marked.

Conversion Leaks

Missing click-to-call, no quote form, no reviews widget, slow load — the specific gaps you can sell the fix for.

Decision Maker

Owner or manager name and title where publicly findable. Marked Not Found when it isn't — never fabricated.

Scores & Grade

Urgency / intent score, project-value opportunity, and the final A, B, or C grade. Sorted grade-first so your best targets are on top.

FAQ

What format is the data in?

CSV — opens in Excel, Google Sheets, or any CRM that imports CSV. 31 columns, one row per business, sorted by grade then opportunity.

How fresh is the data?

Each pack is researched and verified before listing. Businesses are confirmed operating at research time; contact details follow strict Not Found / Not verified rules rather than guesses.

What do the A, B, C grades mean?

A — strongest pitch targets: clear need plus reachable decision maker. B — solid businesses worth contacting, lighter urgency signals. C — valid operating businesses with thinner data or lower opportunity. Every pack lists its grade mix up front.

Can I share or resell the pack?

Your purchase is licensed to you / your agency for outreach use. Reselling or redistributing the raw files isn't permitted.

What if a number is disconnected?

It happens — businesses change numbers. Where we found conflicting numbers during research, it's flagged in the data. If you find a dead contact, tell us and we'll make it right on your next pack.
