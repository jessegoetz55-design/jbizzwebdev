## Email 3 — Day 3
**Subject options:**
- The 3 mistakes I see on almost every site I audit
- Your site might be doing #2 right now

Hey [Name],

I've audited a lot of small-business sites. Same three mistakes, almost every time:

**1. No proof anywhere near the buy button.**
Testimonials buried on a "Reviews" page nobody visits. Your best review should be within eyeshot of every CTA.

**2. "Contact us" is the only option.**
That's a big ask for a cold visitor. A free checklist, a quiz, a quick audit — give the not-ready buyers a smaller yes, or they just leave.

**3. No follow-up exists.**
97% of visitors don't convert on the first visit. If you're not capturing emails and following up automatically, you're paying for traffic twice — once to get them, once to get them back.

How many of these did your checklist flag?

If you want, I can run the full interactive version of the audit on your site — it scores you 0–100 and ranks your top 5 fixes by impact. Takes me 20 minutes. Free.

Just reply with your URL.

— Jesse
